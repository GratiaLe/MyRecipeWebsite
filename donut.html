Classic Glazed Donuts

Prep Time: 25 mins

Fry Time: 15 mins

Servings: 12 Donuts

Ingredients

  • 500g All-Purpose Flour
  • 250ml Warm Milk
  • 7g Active Dry Yeast
  • 50g Sugar
  • 60g Unsalted Butter (Melted)
  • 2 Large Eggs
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • Oil for frying
  • 200g Powdered Sugar (for glaze)
  • 3-4 tbsp Milk or Heavy Cream (for glaze)
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract (for glaze)

Instructions

  1. Combine warm milk, yeast, and sugar in a bowl; let sit for 5 minutes until frothy.
  2. Add flour, eggs, melted butter, and salt; mix and knead into a smooth dough.
  3. Cover and allow the dough to rise in a warm spot for 1 hour.
  4. Roll dough out to 1/2 inch thickness and cut out donut shapes.
  5. Let cut donuts rise for 20 minutes while heating oil to 175°C (350°F).
  6. Fry donuts for 1-2 minutes per side until golden brown, then drain on paper towels.
  7. Whisk powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla together in a small bowl.
  8. Dip warm donuts directly into the glaze and set on a wire rack to set.
