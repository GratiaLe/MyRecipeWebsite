Chocolate Éclairs

Prep Time: 30 mins

Bake Time: 30 mins

Servings: 10 Éclairs

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups pastry cream or vanilla pudding
  • 100g dark chocolate (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Bring butter and water to a boil in a saucepan. Stir in flour all at once until a dough ball forms. Remove from heat.
  3. Beat in eggs one at a time until the dough is smooth and glossy.
  4. Pipe 4-inch logs of dough onto the baking sheet and bake for 25-30 minutes until hollow and golden brown. Let cool completely.
  5. Heat heavy cream until hot, pour over chopped chocolate, and stir until smooth to make the glaze.
  6. Slice eclairs horizontally or poke holes at the ends, pipe in pastry cream, and dip the top of each eclair in the warm chocolate glaze.
