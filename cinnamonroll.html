Cinnamon Rolls

Prep Time: 30 mins

Bake Time: 25 mins

Servings: 12 Rolls

Ingredients

  • 500g All-Purpose Flour
  • 250ml Warm Milk
  • 7g Active Dry Yeast
  • 50g Granulated Sugar
  • 75g Unsalted Butter (Melted)
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 150g Brown Sugar & 2 tbsp Cinnamon (for filling)

Instructions

  1. Mix warm milk, yeast, and sugar; let sit for 5 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add flour, melted butter, and salt; knead into a smooth dough.
  3. Cover and let rise in a warm place for 1 hour until doubled in size.
  4. Roll dough into a rectangle, spread butter over it, and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar.
  5. Roll tightly into a log, slice into 12 equal rolls, and place in a baking dish.
  6. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 20 to 25 minutes until golden brown.
