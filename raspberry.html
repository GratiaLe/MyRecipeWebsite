Raspberry Danish

Prep Time: 20 mins

Bake Time: 18 mins

Servings: 8 Danishes

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry (thawed)
  • 115g cream cheese (softened)
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup raspberry jam or preserve
  • 1/2 cup fresh raspberries
  • 1 egg (beaten, for egg wash)
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar & 1 tbsp milk (for drizzle)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Beat cream cheese, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract until smooth.
  3. Unroll puff pastry and cut into 8 equal rectangles. Score a border inside each piece.
  4. Spread a tablespoon of cream cheese mixture inside the border of each rectangle.
  5. Spoon raspberry jam over cream cheese and top with a few fresh raspberries.
  6. Brush pastry edges with beaten egg and bake for 15-18 minutes until golden and puffed.
  7. Whisk powdered sugar and milk together, then drizzle over cooled pastries before serving.
