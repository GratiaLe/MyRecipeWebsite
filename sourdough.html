Sourdough Bread

Prep Time: 30 mins

Bake Time: 45 mins

Servings: 1 Loaf

Ingredients

  • 500g Bread Flour
  • 350g Water
  • 100g Sourdough Starter
  • 10g Salt

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, water, starter, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Stretch and fold the dough every 30 minutes for 2 hours.
  3. Let the dough rise until doubled in size.
  4. Shape into a loaf and rest in the fridge overnight.
  5. Bake in a Dutch oven at 230°C for 45 minutes.
