Custard Tarts

Prep Time: 20 mins

Bake Time: 25 mins

Servings: 12 Tarts

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry (thawed)
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Ground nutmeg or cinnamon (for topping)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F) and grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
  2. Whisk egg yolks, sugar, and cornstarch in a saucepan until smooth. Gradually whisk in milk.
  3. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens into a smooth custard. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla extract.
  4. Roll out puff pastry, cut into 12 circles, and press each into a muffin tin cup.
  5. Pour the custard into the pastry shells and dust lightly with nutmeg or cinnamon.
  6. Bake for 20-25 minutes until the pastry is golden and the custard tops are slightly browned. Allow to cool before serving.
