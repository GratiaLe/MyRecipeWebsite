Chocolate Cake

Prep Time: 20 mins

Bake Time: 35 mins

Servings: 8-10 Slices

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 cup boiling water or hot coffee

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease two 9-inch round cake pans.
  2. Whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
  3. Add eggs, milk, and oil, beating on medium speed for about 2 minutes until smooth.
  4. Stir in boiling water by hand (the batter will be very thin).
  5. Pour batter evenly into the prepared pans.
  6. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool completely before frosting.
